What We Do

Our Services

From on-site surveys to strategic risk consultancy, we provide the full spectrum of risk intelligence services — tailored to your specific needs.

Survey Services

Comprehensive Survey Solutions

Our surveying capability spans on-site physical inspections, desktop and virtual assessments, and cutting-edge drone surveys — giving you the right type of intelligence for every risk profile and budget.

Each survey is carried out by qualified, experienced professionals who understand the commercial realities of insurance and the underwriting decisions that follow.

Survey
Physical Inspection

On-Site Surveys

Our most comprehensive survey offering. A qualified surveyor attends the premises to carry out a thorough physical inspection, producing a detailed risk report aligned to underwriter requirements.

  • COPE Surveys — Construction, Occupancy, Protection & Exposure assessed in full
  • Pre-Cover Surveys — risk intelligence before inception or renewal
  • Mid-term and Claims-driven surveys
  • Policyholder compliance and clause verification
  • Loss estimates and MFL/PML assessments
  • Risk improvement recommendations with follow-up
Remote Assessment

Desktop / Virtual Surveys

A cost-effective, fast-turnaround alternative for lower-risk properties or where rapid assessment is needed. Our desktop surveys draw on open-source intelligence, satellite imagery, and structured virtual interviews with the policyholder.

  • Remote risk profiling using satellite, aerial, and mapping data
  • Structured virtual interviews with key contacts on-site
  • Occupancy and operational risk assessment
  • Available as a standalone report or as a complement to on-site surveys
  • Rapid turnaround — ideal for time-sensitive underwriting decisions
UAV Technology

Drone Surveys

Using UAV (Unmanned Aerial Vehicle) technology, our drone surveys provide detailed aerial intelligence of complex or large-footprint sites — capturing roof condition, perimeter, exposure, and site layout with exceptional clarity.

  • High-resolution aerial imagery of the full site and roof structures
  • Assessment of roof integrity, guttering, and drainage condition
  • Perimeter and exposure mapping
  • Ideal for large industrial, commercial, and multi-building sites
  • Compliant with CAA regulations — fully insured operators
  • Reduces need for costly scaffolding or manual roof inspections
Consultancy

Risk Management & Consultancy

Beyond the survey, we act as strategic risk partners — providing ongoing consultancy that helps businesses understand, manage, and reduce their risk profile over time.

Our consultancy services are designed to create lasting value for both insurers and policyholders, reducing loss frequency and supporting sustainable portfolios.

Consult

Risk Consultancy

We work alongside underwriters, brokers, and risk managers to deliver expert technical guidance on complex or high-value risks. Our consultants bring deep sector knowledge and practical experience to every engagement.

Whether you need support on a single complex account or ongoing strategic input across a portfolio, our consultancy offering is built around your needs.

  • Pre-underwriting risk review and intelligence
  • Portfolio risk analysis and benchmarking
  • Technical guidance on complex industrial and commercial risks
  • Risk improvement programme design and management
  • Clause and endorsement drafting support
  • Expert support for claims and loss investigations
  • Bespoke training for underwriting and broking teams

Aftercare

A survey report is only as valuable as the action taken. Our aftercare service ensures that risk improvement recommendations are tracked, followed up, and completed — creating a continuous feedback loop that keeps insurers informed and policyholders accountable.

This service is particularly valued by underwriters who want to demonstrate proactive portfolio management and by brokers seeking to add tangible value to their client relationships.

  • Structured follow-up on outstanding risk improvement actions
  • Evidence collection and documentation of improvements made
  • Periodic re-survey to verify compliance
  • Progress reporting to underwriters and brokers
  • Ongoing dialogue with policyholders on risk management best practice
  • Support through mid-term changes, extensions, and renewals

One of the most complex and rapidly evolving sectors in commercial insurance. Waste facilities present significant fire, environmental, and operational hazards — making expert surveying essential for accurate risk assessment.

Biomass & Renewable Energy

The energy transition is creating new and complex risk profiles for insurers. Biomass facilities, solar farms, battery storage, and anaerobic digestion plants require specialist risk understanding beyond conventional property surveys.

We assess combustion risks, feedstock hazards, process failure scenarios, and the unique protection challenges of renewable energy installations — helping underwriters price these risks with confidence.

Liability & Casualty Practice

Expert Liability Risk Assessment & Advisory

Commercial liability is evolving rapidly. Social inflation, emerging risks, and increasingly complex supply chains are creating pressure on casualty portfolios that conventional underwriting tools weren't designed for.

Our Liability & Casualty practice brings specialist knowledge to bear on the risks that matter — helping insurers, underwriters, and brokers understand their exposure before it crystallises into a claim.

Led by Simon, our team works closely with liability underwriters to deliver pre-underwriting risk intelligence, policy review, and claims support — covering everything from employer's liability and public liability to product liability and professional indemnity adjacent risks.

We believe that better liability underwriting starts with better risk understanding — not just at inception, but throughout the policy lifecycle.

🏗 Employer's & Public Liability

Workplace hazard assessment, public interface risk, and compliance review — supporting accurate EL/PL underwriting decisions.

📦 Product Liability

Supply chain risk assessment, product recall exposure, and compliance with product safety regulations across manufacturing and distribution.

🚧 Contractors & Operations

Risk profiling for construction, contracting, and operational businesses — including method statement review and site hazard assessment.

⚖️ Claims Support & Advisory

Post-incident advisory and forensic risk review to support insurers and legal teams in understanding cause and quantum of liability losses.

🌍 Environmental Liability

Environmental impairment and pollution liability risk assessment — critical for industrial, waste, and energy sector clients.

📊 Portfolio Analytics

Casualty portfolio review and benchmarking — identifying systemic risk concentrations and improvement opportunities across books of business.

Liability Insights

Our perspective on
the liability landscape

📌 Key Theme

Social Inflation & Claims Severity

The steady increase in claims severity driven by evolving litigation strategies, third-party litigation funding, and increased plaintiff advocacy is reshaping casualty portfolios. Understanding the underlying risk at inception is more important than ever.

📌 Key Theme

Supply Chain & Product Risk

Globalised supply chains have created product liability exposures that are difficult to trace and harder to price. Our team helps insurers identify where in the chain the real risk sits — and ensure that coverage terms reflect the actual exposure profile.

📌 Key Theme

Emerging & Systemic Risks

PFAS contamination, autonomous systems liability, and AI-related product failures are among the emerging casualty risks beginning to crystallise in insurance portfolios. We help clients think ahead — not just react.

Beyond the Mainstream

Specialist Areas & Risks

Some risks demand deeper expertise than a standard survey can provide. Our specialist capability covers the most complex, technical, and niche risk classes in the market.

Specialist Capability

Where standard approaches
don't go far enough

Complex risks require specialist thinking. Our team includes surveyors and consultants with backgrounds in engineering, loss control, environmental science, and industry operations — enabling us to go beyond the surface.

01

Battery Energy Storage (BESS)

Lithium-ion and advanced chemistry battery storage presents unique thermal runaway, fire, and environmental contamination risks. We provide specialist BESS risk assessments for underwriters navigating this fast-growing exposure class.

02

Hazardous Materials & COSHH

Chemical storage, processing, and handling — including COMAH-regulated sites, solvent users, and flammable liquid storage. Full assessment of containment, segregation, and emergency response capability.

03

Fire Protection Engineering

In-depth assessment of sprinkler systems, suppression systems, detection, compartmentation, and fire strategy — going beyond a visual check to assess design adequacy and maintenance standards.

04

Business Interruption & Contingency

BI exposure analysis — assessing dependencies, single points of failure, supply chain vulnerability, and the adequacy of BI sum insured and indemnity periods relative to actual recovery timescales.

05

Heritage & Listed Buildings

Specialist survey methodology for listed and heritage properties — understanding construction vulnerabilities, reinstatement complications, and the unique exposures of historic fabric.

06

Flood & Climate Resilience

Physical climate risk assessment for individual properties and portfolios — flood pathway analysis, drainage adequacy, resilience measures, and alignment with emerging climate-related disclosure requirements.

07

Arson & Crime Risk

Security risk assessment covering physical perimeter, access controls, CCTV, alarm systems, and target-hardening recommendations — particularly relevant for vacant properties, high-value stock, and isolated rural sites.

08

High-Value Residential

Specialist surveys for high-net-worth residential properties — assessing construction, security, contents vulnerability, and bespoke risk features that fall outside standard residential survey frameworks.

09

Vacant & Unoccupied Property

Heightened risk assessment for vacant commercial and residential properties — covering arson, escape of water, structural deterioration, and compliance with vacancy conditions and clauses.
