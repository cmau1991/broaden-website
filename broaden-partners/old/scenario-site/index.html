About Us

Expert-led risk surveys
& technical consultancy

We provide expert-led risk surveys across the UK and Ireland, offering deep alignment with underwriter needs and fast turnaround with consistently high-quality reporting.

Risk Surveys

Comprehensive surveys tailored to multiple insurance classes — on-site, desktop, drone, or virtual. From COPE evaluations to pre-cover assessments.

COPE Evaluations

Detailed Construction, Occupancy, Protection, and Exposure assessments providing underwriters with the clarity they need to price risk accurately.

Compliance Assessment

Assessment of policyholder compliance with terms, clauses, and endorsements — identifying gaps before they become claims.

Risk Improvement

Customised risk improvement plans with practical, actionable recommendations to reduce risk and enhance long-term portfolio performance.

Loss Estimation

Accurate loss estimates grounded in site intelligence and underwriting expertise — supporting reserving, pricing, and risk transfer decisions.

Consultancy & Aftercare

Ongoing risk consultancy support and aftercare — we don’t just survey and leave. We’re strategic partners throughout the policy lifecycle.
