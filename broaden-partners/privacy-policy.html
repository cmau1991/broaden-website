Privacy Policy

How we handle your data.

How Broaden Partners collects, uses, shares and protects personal data for candidates, clients, suppliers and website visitors.

Last updated: Oct 23, 2026 by Oliver Gordon-Brown

1. Who we are

Broaden Management Partners ("we", "us", "our") is a recruitment business that introduces candidates to clients for permanent, contract and temporary roles. We are committed to protecting the personal data of everyone we work with.

This policy explains how we collect, use, share and protect personal data in line with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. It applies to candidates, clients, suppliers, referees and visitors to our website.

Broaden Management Partners is the data controller for the personal data we process.

  • Registered address: 8-9 New Street, EC2M 4TP
  • ICO registration number: ZC079547
  • Data protection contact: Oliver Gordon-Brown, Founder

2. The personal data we collect

Candidates

  • Name, contact details, date of birth and address
  • CV, employment history, education, qualifications and skills
  • Current and desired salary, notice period and job preferences
  • Right-to-work documents and identity checks
  • References, interview notes and assessment results
  • Bank, tax and National Insurance details (contract and temporary workers only)
  • Any information you choose to share with us during the recruitment process

We may also process special category data, such as health information needed to make reasonable adjustments, or diversity monitoring data you choose to provide. Where a role requires it and the law allows, we may process criminal record information from background checks. We only do this where necessary and with appropriate safeguards.

Clients and suppliers

  • Names, job titles and business contact details of our contacts
  • Details of roles, hiring requirements and feedback on candidates
  • Billing and payment information

Website visitors

  • IP address, browser type, device information and pages visited
  • Information you submit through forms, job applications or email sign-ups

3. How we collect it

  • Directly from you — when you apply for a role, send us your CV, register on our website, speak to a consultant or attend an interview.
  • From third parties — job boards, professional networking sites (such as LinkedIn), referees, background-check providers and clients who give feedback on you.
  • From public sources — professional profiles and information you have made publicly available, where relevant to recruitment.
  • Automatically — through cookies and similar technologies when you use our website.

If we obtain your details from a third party or public source, we will tell you within one month and give you a link to this policy.

4. How we use it and our lawful basis

We only use personal data where the law gives us a valid reason to do so.

Purpose Lawful basis
Matching candidates to roles and introducing them to clients Legitimate interests; contract (where you have asked us to represent you)
Arranging interviews and managing the recruitment process Legitimate interests; contract
Right-to-work, identity and background checks Legal obligation; legitimate interests
Paying contract and temporary workers, and handling tax Contract; legal obligation
Managing client relationships and invoicing Contract; legitimate interests
Sending job alerts and marketing emails Consent (or legitimate interests for existing contacts, with an easy opt-out)
Running and improving our website Legitimate interests; consent for non-essential cookies
Complying with laws, regulators and legal claims Legal obligation; legitimate interests

Where we rely on legitimate interests, our interest is running an effective recruitment business that helps candidates find work and clients find staff. We balance this against your rights and will not use your data in ways you would not reasonably expect.

We process special category and criminal record data only where permitted, for example to meet employment law obligations, or with your explicit consent. You can withdraw consent at any time.

We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects.

5. Who we share it with

We never sell your personal data. We share it only where necessary, with:

  • Clients — prospective employers or hirers, when we introduce you for a role. We will discuss a role with you before sending your CV.
  • Service providers — such as our applicant tracking system, IT and cloud hosting, payroll, background-check and email providers. They act on our instructions under written contracts.
  • Professional advisers — lawyers, accountants, auditors and insurers.
  • Authorities — HMRC, the Home Office, law enforcement or regulators, where the law requires it.
  • A buyer or successor — if our business is sold, merged or restructured.

International transfers

Some of our service providers or clients may be based outside the UK. Where we transfer personal data outside the UK, we make sure it is protected by a UK adequacy decision, the International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, or another lawful safeguard. Contact us for more details.

6. How long we keep it and how we protect it

We keep personal data only as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as the law requires.

Data Typical retention period
Candidate records with no recent contact 2 years from our last meaningful contact with you
Records of placed candidates and workers 6 years after the end of the placement (tax and legal claims)
Right-to-work checks Duration of the engagement plus 2 years
Client and supplier records 6 years after the end of the relationship
Marketing preferences Until you unsubscribe or withdraw consent

When data is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymise it.

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including access controls, encryption, secure systems and staff training. If a data breach occurs that is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify you and the ICO as required by law.

7. Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erase your data in certain circumstances
  • Restrict how we use your data
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time
  • Data portability — receive your data in a reusable format
  • Withdraw consent where we rely on it

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@broaden.partners. We will respond within one month. There is usually no charge.

8. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to make it work and, with your consent, to understand how visitors use it. You can manage your preferences through our cookie banner or your browser settings. See our Cookie Policy for details.

9. Complaints

If you have a concern about how we handle your data, please contact us first so we can try to resolve it. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on our website, with the date it was last updated at the top.
